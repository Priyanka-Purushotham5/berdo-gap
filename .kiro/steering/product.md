# Product

BERDO Gap estimates how much a large Boston building will owe each year under BERDO's emissions limits (the $234-per-ton Alternative Compliance Payment), shows which retrofit removes that exposure, and drafts a one-page memo for the owner's board.

## Users
- Property managers and condo boards of BERDO-covered buildings (non-residential ≥ 20,000 sq ft; residential ≥ 15 units).
- City of Boston environment staff choosing where to send outreach.

## Never claim
- Never say a building "is in violation" or "will be fined." Say "estimated exposure" and "estimate, not a compliance determination."
- Never show a number that did not come from the calculator or the City's data file.
- Never give legal or financial advice; point to the City's BERDO team for official answers.
- Portfolio and campus compliance, flexibility measures and a cleaner electric grid can all lower real exposure; say so on every result screen.

## Sources to cite in the UI
- Limits and ACP: boston.gov BERDO page.
- Building data: City of Boston BERDO 2026 disclosure (2025 data), Analyze Boston, PDDL license.
